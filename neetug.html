About RN Counselling

NEET UG Admission Guidance, Made Clearer

RN Counselling helps students understand their medical admission options and the steps involved in counselling. We provide course-related resources, college prediction tools and counselling packages to support more informed decisions.

Our aim is to make the process easier to understand—from exploring colleges and reviewing counselling routes to preparing choices based on each student's rank, eligibility, preferences and budget.

✓ Rank-focused guidance ✓ College and course options ✓ Counselling resources ✓ Clear package information
Make informed decisions

Why Counselling Guidance Matters

Medical admission counselling involves multiple rounds, quotas, eligibility rules, deadlines and choice-filling decisions.

Why Counselling Is Important

  • Understand applicable counselling routes and quota options.
  • Compare colleges according to rank, preferences and budget.
  • Review eligibility requirements and key deadlines.
  • Plan choices carefully and reduce avoidable process mistakes.
  • Understand documents and steps required during admission.

How RN Counselling Can Help

  • Explore course-specific admission information and resources.
  • Use the college predictor to explore possible options.
  • Review package inclusions before choosing a service.
  • Get guidance aligned with your rank and stated preferences.
  • Find important updates and contact support when needed.

Quick answers

NEET UG Counselling FAQs

Which courses are covered on this page?

This page links to information for MBBS, BDS, AYUSH and other listed course areas. Availability and eligibility depend on the course and relevant admission authority.

Does the college predictor guarantee a seat?

No. Predictor results are estimates. Actual allotment depends on rank, category, eligibility, choices, seat availability, rules and the counselling authority's decisions.

Where can I check counselling packages and prices?

Use the Compare All Packages button to see the current package options, inclusions and prices before applying.

Where should I check official counselling dates?

Use our notifications page as a starting point, then verify important dates, eligibility and rules on the official website of the relevant counselling authority.

How can I contact RN Counselling?

You can call or message us on WhatsApp using the contact buttons on this page.
